## Projects & Contribution
- **Timestep Score Adaptive Distillation**: developing a timestep-aware diffusion distillation approach that adaptively weights training based on the teacher model’s score reliability across timesteps.
- **Reinforcement Learning Infrastructure and Policy Training for 12-DOF Quadruped**: Building an end-to-end reinforcement learning pipeline for a custom 12-DOF quadruped, covering simulation, control, environment design, and policy training.
- **Finding Belief in Physics**: Explored how neural networks can learn and update beliefs about physical systems, combining machine learning with physics-based reasoning.
- **Reproducibility Study on CR-JEPA**: Reproduced and evaluated the CR-JEPA framework with limited compute and datatset, understand its training pipeline, and investigate implementation-level factors affecting performance.
- **Playing with DDPM**: Implemented and experimented with Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models (DDPMs) from scratch, exploring the fundamentals of diffusion-based generative modeling and sampling.
