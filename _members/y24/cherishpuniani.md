## Research
- **[B-DENSE](https://cherishpuniani.github.io/research/b-dense/)** (ICLR DeLTa 2026; [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.15971))
  - Developed dense trajectory supervision for diffusion distillation, improving low-NFE sampling quality on CIFAR-10 and ImageNet (64×64) with 0.01% computational overhead.
  - Multiple output branches share a U-Net backbone and learn intermediate teacher states. CIFAR-10 gains span the tested sampling budgets; ImageNet improvements depend on the step count, with gains at two evaluations and mixed results at higher budgets.
- **[Image-Alchemy](https://cherishpuniani.github.io/research/image-alchemy/)** (ICLR DeLTa 2025; [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.10743))
  - Built a two-stage subject-driven personalization framework for SDXL using LoRA fine-tuning and segmentation-guided isolation.
  - Identified 28 placeholder tokens through CLIP embedding analysis and achieved a DINO identity-fidelity score of 0.789.
  - Separates scene composition from subject insertion using Grounding DINO and SAM. The strongest reported result is DINO subject similarity; other image-quality measures are mixed.
- **[Impact of Language Guidance: A Reproducibility Study](https://cherishpuniani.github.io/research/impact-of-language-guidance/)** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.08140), 2025)
  - Reproduced over 80% of baseline experiments using BLIP-2-generated captions and measured the effect of ConvNet architecture on representation quality.
  - Studied language-guided positive-pair sampling in SimCLR and SimSiam with a ResNet-34 backbone, using image-text matching to refine RedCaps captions and evaluating linear probes, few-shot classification, and saliency maps.
